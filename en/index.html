Where launches usually break

I see the same three failures again and again. Each one costs sign-ups, revenue or nerves.

  • Problem

    Sign-ups get lost between the form and the CRM.

    Fix

    Flows with checks and retries that stay reliable even when many people sign up at once.

  • Problem

    Launch emails land in spam.

    Fix

    Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC correctly and test delivery.

  • Problem

    The instalment payment failed, but access is still open.

    Fix

    Automatic reminders, suspension and reactivation, in sync with Quaderno, Somba or Stripe.
