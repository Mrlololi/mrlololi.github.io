Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what I am asked most often.

Question not here? → Write to me
Do I need to know about tech?

No. You tell me what should happen, I take care of the rest and explain everything in plain words.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Almost all projects run remotely across Europe. In Graz I am happy to meet in person.

How soon can you start?

Quick fixes often within a week. Projects by arrangement, because I take on only a few at a time.

Do I need specific tools?

No. I work with what you already have and only suggest a switch when it pays off.

How do you handle data?

I get my own user access instead of your password. When the project ends, you remove my access and I delete the copies of your data that I hold.

Do you also do customer support?

No. I build the tech that takes load off your support, for example draft replies for recurring questions.

Are you available on launch day?

Yes, by phone and chat in a time window we agree beforehand: for example two hours from the start of sales and the last two hours before cart close. I bill this window by effort (€45 per hour). Outside it I reply by 3 pm the next day. We catch most problems beforehand: with test contacts, test purchases and an emergency card with ready answers for your customers. I support at most one launch per week, so that I am really there.

What if my problem doesn't fit a package?

Send me a short note on what isn't working. You get the price before I start.

I use a different tool. Does that work?

Most course platforms connect via ActiveCampaign, Zapier or Make, for example Digistore24, CopeCart, ablefy, Kajabi or Memberspot. In the tech check I look at your tool first and tell you honestly whether I can help.

Is there a data processing agreement?

Yes. Before the project starts we sign a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR (the WKO template), including the sub-processors.

And what if you are ill?

Then I still help: almost everything can be solved remotely from my laptop. Also, all accesses belong to you, and every flow is documented.

My setup already exists, but it's messy. Do you clean up too?

Yes. I check tags, automations and Zaps, clean them up and document what happens where. The best start is the tech check.
