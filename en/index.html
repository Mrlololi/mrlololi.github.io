Do I need to know about tech?

No. You tell me what should happen, I take care of the rest and explain everything in plain words.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Almost all projects run remotely across Europe. In Graz I am happy to meet in person.

How soon can you start?

Quick fixes usually within a week. Projects by arrangement, because I take on only a few at a time.

Do I need specific tools?

No. I work with what you already have and only suggest a switch when it pays off.

How do you handle data?

I get my own user access instead of your password. When the project ends, you remove my access and I delete the copies of your data that I hold.

Do you also do customer support?

No. I build the tech that takes load off your support, for example draft replies for recurring questions.

Are you available on launch day?

Yes, by arrangement: we agree on the launch day and a time window beforehand. Before that we test every payment case in test mode.
